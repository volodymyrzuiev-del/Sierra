Who We Are

Our Origin

Sierra Logistics Group was founded to bridge the widening gap in the modern supply chain. In an industry increasingly dominated by impersonal tech giants and under-resourced shops, the human element has been lost.

We believe in investing in the right people and utilizing state-of-the-art technology to empower them. By letting our digital tools handle the complexities of the market, our team is placed in the best possible position to provide 24/7 dedication and high-touch support in a fast-paced world.

Mission Statement

"To deliver white-glove service as the standard, not the exception, by marrying elite human talent with precision technology. We are dedicated to earning your trust through fair, up-to-the-minute pricing and total transparency, ensuring you always know where you stand with us while fostering a family culture that values people as much as performance."

Vision Statement

To build a cohesive national network of logistics hubs across major U.S. cities, providing total transportation solutions that offer the scale and reach of a global leader with the agility and personal touch of a boutique partner.
